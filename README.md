The focus of the project is to create a cost-effective, lightweight, and functional prosthetic arm that provides navigational awareness using myoelectric control and deep learning. Its goal is to assist amputees by offering affordable and economical solutions that mimic the natural hand. The approach integrates EMG sensors to detect muscle activity, a NodeMCU microcontroller for signal processing, and deep learning models for gesture recognition. The servo motor and fishing line create muscle-like mechanisms that make the game more competitive and realist. The design was modeled using Autodesk Fusion 360 and manufactured using 3D printing to ensure value and ergonomic comfort. The design features instant interactive sensory feedback and a rechargeable power system.
Prosthetics address the limitations of current solutions, such as high cost, limited functionality, and user discomfort. Its innovative approach offers another way to reach underserved populations, improving quality of life, and supporting cultural practices through additive manufacturing.
Combining advanced technology with user-friendly design, solutions bridge the gap between limb functionality and affordability
